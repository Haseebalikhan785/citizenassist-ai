# Multilingual Localization Review (i18n)

> **Notice Regarding Machine-Generated Translations**

CitizenAssist AI supports **English**, **Hindi (हिंदी)**, and **Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ)** across the user interface, navigation, category cards, and assistant interactions.

## Transparency Disclosure
- The interface translation strings located in `frontend/src/i18n/hi.json` and `frontend/src/i18n/pa.json` were **machine-translated and synthetically generated** for the purpose of the GRAVITON 2026 Hackathon prototype.
- While care was taken to utilize appropriate administrative terminology (such as *ਯੋਗਤਾ*, *ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ*, *ਪਾਤਰਤਾ*, *ਕਲਿਆਣਕਾਰੀ ਯੋਜਨਾ*), grammatical nuances or colloquial phrasing should undergo formal human audit by native Punjabi and Hindi linguists prior to production citizen deployment.

## Official Legal Terms Policy
To prevent civic confusion or distortion of legal rights:
- Stored official statutory text (such as scheme names and legal criteria excerpts) are preserved in their original verified form with transliterations and localized explanations alongside.
- We deliberately **do not machine-translate raw legal statutes on the fly** without displaying the original government source text alongside.
