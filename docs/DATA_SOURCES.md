# CitizenAssist AI - Verified Government Data Sources

This document transparently lists all government schemes and citizen services seeded into CitizenAssist AI, including their verification sources, extracted criteria, and official portal URLs.

## Guiding Data Integrity Principles (Section 3 of Brief)
1. **Never invent data**: All schemes, benefits, documents, and rules are taken directly from official government documentation.
2. **Mandatory citations**: Every record maintains its official portal URL, department name, and exact excerpt from source text.
3. **Demo status declaration**: All hackathon seed data is explicitly labeled with verification status `demo` and carries a visible "DEMO DATA" badge, reminding citizens to always verify on the official portal.

## Seeded Government Welfare Schemes (10 Records)

### 1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Category**: Agriculture and Farmers
- **Level**: Central Sector Scheme
- **Official Source**: [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: ₹6,000 per year transferred in three equal installments of ₹2,000 directly to Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Landholding farmer families possessing cultivable land.
  - Exclusions: Institutional landholders, serving/retired government employees, and income-tax payers.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card, Land Records (Jamabandi/Patta), Bank Account details.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 2. Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)
- **Category**: Healthcare
- **Level**: Central Sector Scheme
- **Official Source**: [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/)
- **Verified Benefits**: Cashless health cover up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across empaneled hospitals.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Families listed in SECC 2011 deprivation criteria.
  - Universal coverage for all senior citizens aged 70+ without income criteria.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card, Ration Card / Family ID.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 3. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)
- **Category**: Housing
- **Level**: Central Sector Scheme
- **Official Source**: [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: Financial assistance from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹2.67 lakh through credit-linked interest subsidy or direct construction assistance for pucca house.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Annual household income <= ₹3,00,000 (EWS) or <= ₹6,00,000 (LIG).
  - Family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, Bank Passbook, Affidavit declaring no existing pucca house.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 4. Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students
- **Category**: Education and Scholarships
- **Level**: Centrally Sponsored Scheme
- **Official Source**: [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: Full reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees plus annual maintenance allowance up to ₹13,500.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Applicant must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC).
  - Actively pursuing post-matric studies (Class 11, 12, ITI, Diploma, UG, PG).
  - Total parental/family income <= ₹2,50,000 per annum.
- **Documents Required**: Caste Certificate (SC), Income Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Marksheet of qualifying exam, College Fee receipt.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 5. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
- **Category**: Women and Child Welfare
- **Level**: Central Government Scheme
- **Official Source**: [indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: 8.2% p.a. sovereign-backed tax-free compound interest (EEE status), mature upon 21 years or marriage after age 18.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Opened for girl child before attaining 10 years of age.
  - Maximum 2 accounts per family.
- **Documents Required**: Birth Certificate of Girl Child, Aadhaar Card of Guardian, PAN Card.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 6. Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- **Category**: Social Security
- **Level**: Central Government Scheme
- **Official Source**: [npscra.nsdl.co.in](https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 starting from age 60 for unorganized sector workers.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Age between 18 and 40 years at enrollment.
  - Non-income tax payer.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card, Savings Bank Account with auto-debit consent.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 7. PM Vishwakarma Scheme
- **Category**: Employment
- **Level**: Central Sector Scheme
- **Official Source**: [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: PM Vishwakarma Certificate & ID, ₹500/day training stipend, ₹15,000 toolkit incentive, and collateral-free loans up to ₹3 lakh at 5% interest.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Engaged in one of 18 traditional artisan trades (carpenters, blacksmiths, potters, etc.).
  - Minimum age 18 years.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account Details, Ration Card.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 8. Punjab Ashirwad Scheme (Shagun Scheme)
- **Category**: Financial Assistance
- **Level**: State Government (Punjab)
- **Official Source**: [punjab.gov.in](https://punjab.gov.in) / [eservices.punjab.gov.in](https://eservices.punjab.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: Direct grant of ₹51,000 transferred to bride's bank account for wedding expenses.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Permanent resident / domicile of Punjab.
  - Girl aged 18 or older.
  - Belongs to SC/BC or Economically Weaker Section with annual family income <= ₹32,790.
- **Documents Required**: Domicile Certificate of Punjab, Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, Age Proof, Bank Passbook.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 9. Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
- **Category**: Financial Assistance
- **Level**: Central Sector Scheme
- **Official Source**: [pmuy.gov.in](https://pmuy.gov.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: Free LPG connection with deposit waiver, first 14.2kg cylinder refill free, and free hotplate/stove.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Adult woman (age >= 18) from BPL household.
  - No existing LPG connection in the household.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar Card of Woman Applicant, Ration Card, Bank Passbook.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 10. National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS)
- **Category**: Social Security
- **Level**: Central Government (NSAP)
- **Official Source**: [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in)
- **Verified Benefits**: One-time grant of ₹20,000 paid to surviving head of BPL family on demise of primary breadwinner.
- **Eligibility Criteria Checked**:
  - Primary breadwinner died between ages 18 and 59.
  - Certified Below Poverty Line (BPL).
- **Documents Required**: Death Certificate, BPL Proof / Ration Card, Age Proof of Deceased, Bank Passbook.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

## Seeded Citizen Services (4 Records)

### 1. Issuance of Income Certificate
- **Official Source**: [eservices.punjab.gov.in](https://eservices.punjab.gov.in) / State Revenue Depts
- **Verified Service**: Statutory certificate certifying annual family income from all sources.
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Salary Slip / ITR / Patwari report, Self-declaration affidavit.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 2. Issuance of Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC)
- **Official Source**: [eservices.punjab.gov.in](https://eservices.punjab.gov.in) / Revenue & Social Justice
- **Verified Service**: Statutory document certifying community origin for reservations and scholarships.
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Father's caste certificate or historic residence proof, Passport photo.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 3. Issuance of Domicile / Residence Certificate
- **Official Source**: [eservices.punjab.gov.in](https://eservices.punjab.gov.in) / State Revenue Depts
- **Verified Service**: Official legal proof of continuous permanent residency in the state.
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card / Voter ID, School leaving certificate or continuous utility bills.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`

### 4. Application for Digital Ration Card (NFSA)
- **Official Source**: [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in)
- **Verified Service**: Document enabling subsidized food grains and acting as family composition proof.
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar card of all family members, Address proof, Family group photo.
- **Verification Status**: `demo`
